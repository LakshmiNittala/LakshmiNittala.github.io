SVM Kernel Trick

Prof. Lakshminarayana Nittala, University of Dayton

An interactive visualization to understand how Support Vector Machines use higher dimensions to classify non-linear data.

Select a Dataset

The Challenge

In the 2D view, the two classes of data points (blue and orange) are mixed in a way that makes it impossible to separate them with a single straight line.

The Solution: The "Kernel Trick"

We apply a transformation to map the data into a third dimension. For each point `(x, y)`, we calculate a new `z` coordinate:

z = x² + y²

This "lifts" the points onto a 3D surface based on their distance from the center. This makes the concentric circles separable.

You can rotate the 3D view by clicking and dragging. Zoom with the scroll wheel.
