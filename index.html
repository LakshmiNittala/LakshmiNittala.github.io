About Me

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of MIS, Operations & Supply Chain Management, and Business Analytics at the University of Dayton. My background uniquely bridges engineering precision with business strategy.

I hold two Ph.D. degrees—one in Management (Innovation, Technology & Operations) and another in Materials Science & Engineering. This dual expertise allows me to approach complex operational problems with deep technical and analytical rigor.
