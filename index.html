About Me

I am an Associate Professor in Operations and Business Analytics with a rich background in engineering and technology development. Currently based in Dayton, Ohio, I specialize in applying analytical frameworks to solve complex operational challenges.

My journey began in Materials Engineering, working with industry leaders like Novellus Systems, before transitioning into academia to focus on Innovation, Technology, and Operations.

I am passionate about leveraging Machine Learning and Business Analytics to drive decision-making processes and educate the next generation of data-driven leaders.
